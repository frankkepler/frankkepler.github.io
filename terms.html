Internal-use terms

Duratus Backups is intended only for authorized administration of Duratus website backups. It is not offered as a public backup service, membership service or billing service. Only an authorized administrator should connect an account, access recovery credentials, restore a backup or delete backup records.

The utility itself does not collect payment or charge a subscription. The connected Google account remains subject to Google's own account, storage and service terms. Administrators are responsible for retaining recovery credentials separately, checking backup and restore results, and keeping backup folders private.

Contact the backup administrator at frankkepler@gmail.com for support.
